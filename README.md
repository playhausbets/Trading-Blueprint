# The Trading Blueprint Academy™

*Master the markets with structure, discipline & confidence — by Bria Maddox.*

Built to help people that struggle with options trading.

Free, step-by-step options trading lessons with missions, journal prompts, knowledge checks, badges and a certificate of completion.

**6 modules · 20 lessons**
1. Building Your Foundation — why the market moves, candlesticks, market structure, timeframes, support/resistance & liquidity, trendlines
2. Your Trading Toolkit — the judges panel (EMA 9/21, VWAP, order blocks), TradingView setup
3. Options Essentials — options 101, picking strikes & expirations, IV & earnings
4. Managing Risk & Mindset — managing the trade, risk management
5. Finding High-Probability Entries — the entry flowchart, PDH/PDL, ORB (Breakout-Retest-Entry), fair value gaps, EOD SPX/VIX
6. Income Strategies — cash-secured puts, covered calls & the wheel

**Tools:** trade setup flowchart, daily checklist, risk rules, trade journal + performance dashboard (CSV export), position size, CSP and expectancy calculators.

Everything a visitor enters (progress, journal, notes) is saved only in their own browser.
